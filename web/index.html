Method and limitations

Using the table [[case ALT, case REF], [control ALT, control REF]], the app computes Pearson's χ² statistic with 1 degree of freedom and its asymptotic upper-tail probability, erfc(√(χ²/2)). The odds ratio is (case ALT × control REF) / (case REF × control ALT); a zero denominator is reported as undefined. Expected cell counts below 5 are flagged because the asymptotic approximation can be unreliable.

Allelic analyses on two chromosomes per individual may not be equivalent to genotype-based tests; these results do not adjust for ancestry, relatedness, covariates, multiple comparisons, or linkage disequilibrium. A p-value is not a clinical risk estimate or proof of causality. Cryptographic guarantees are not provided.
